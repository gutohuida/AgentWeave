**First draft blocked globally and hung.** The first version patched `os.path.realpath`
unconditionally for every call, for the whole test. Running it against a throwaway stand-in hung
past the tool's 120s timeout with no output at all -- killed via TaskStop rather than diagnosed

of times around a test body, and delaying every one of them compounds into an unbounded hang
rather than a bounded 2s wait. Rewrote the patch to delay only calls naming this test's own

test's own worst case to one 2s delay and leaves pytest's own realpath traffic at full speed. This
is a caution worth carrying forward: any future case in this file (or elsewhere) that needs to
make a real stdlib call artificially slow should scope the patch to the specific argument under
test, not the function globally, unless every other caller during the test window is known and
accounted for.
