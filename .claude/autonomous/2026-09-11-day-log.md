**A structural note on where the gate check belongs, because it explains why the gate has opened
once ever.** `AgentWeaveDayLoop` fires on `PT5M` and every firing ends in a commit, so at the
*start* of any firing `HEAD` is at most about five minutes old, while `ci.yml`'s `hub-test` job
takes **10–15 minutes**. A gate check placed at the start of a firing therefore finds an in-flight
run essentially always, no matter how many days pass. Placed at the **end** of a long firing —
still before that firing commits, which is what `8596706`'s ordering fix actually requires — the
previous commit has had the whole firing to build. This iteration ran the check at the end and got a
real verdict rather than a wait. The queue below folds the check into the tail of each spec round
instead of spending a firing on it.
