and `PtySession.spawn` imports them lazily, so the first real pty spawn in a worker made under a faked `which` pins the fake for the
whole worker. ptyprocess re-resolves argv[0] itself, which is why an absolute `sys.executable` still became `/usr/bin/claude`.
**Guard:** a `pytest_runtest_teardown` wrapper in `hub/tests/conftest.py` runs after every fixture finalizer, `monkeypatch` included, and
fails the test that leaves `which` changed in `shutil` or a pty backend (it restores the real one first). A synthetic leak test confirmed it fires.
**Finding the leakers:** the Windows full suite at `-n 4 --dist loadfile` was green (7147 passed in 29m54s) and the guard stayed silent,
because on Windows no leaker is the first to spawn. In WSL, the 55 which-patching files run serially named

(`testbed/f530/fresh_pty.py`, untracked) that forgets ptyprocess before every test named 17 more in 8 files. All 18 fire a real trigger
under `patch(...shutil.which, return_value="/usr/bin/claude")` with no spawn stub.
**Fix:** a conftest fixture `no_agent_cli_spawn` (`PtySession.spawn` fails with FileNotFoundError, as the fake path already did, and imports
