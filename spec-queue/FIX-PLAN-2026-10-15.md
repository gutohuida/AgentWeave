The findings are grouped by **shared root**, not by checklist area, because several clusters close with one fix. Streams are ordered by what they cost, money and safety first. There is no calendar: take a stream, finish it, drive it, move on.

## How every fix is run

- **Tier per CLAUDE.md, stated in the first commit.**
  - **Tier 0:** a test that fails at the seam the finding names, then the fix, then a drive, then set the `**Status:**` line.
  - **Tier 1/2:** a short `spec/changes/<name>` document on the trial Hub (`:8010`, `proj-d85a82bf4216`) with an acceptance drive. A Tier 2 change has the acceptance drive written and failing *before* the build.
  - No R1/R2/R3 rounds.
- **The six openspec drafts from the work PC** (`openspec/changes/a-refused-copilot-call…`, `the-workspace-judge-reads-paths…`, `the-copilot-catalog…`, `a-call-file-turn-starts-clean…`, `copilot-spend-is-counted…`, `stop-closes-the-tool-card…`) are **input, not the change**.
  - Re-author each as a short `spec/changes` document when its stream starts.
  - Then `openspec archive <name> --skip-specs --yes`, with a note naming the successor.
  - Their `design.md` option analyses are the decision record; don't copy them in.
- **Drive each stream** with `/e2e-loop` scoped to it, on a scratch Hub (8030+, Haiku). Copilot rows need the work PC: mark them *not reached* at home and carry a drive note there.
- **One commit per finding or change, pushed.** Add a `spec-queue/METRICS.md` row while the trial runs (until ~2026-10-18).
- **New problems found while fixing go to the ledger**, not into the change in hand.
