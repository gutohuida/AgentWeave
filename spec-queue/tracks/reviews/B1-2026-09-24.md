## 1. `a-task-is-attended-only-by-a-turn-that-will-reach-it`: APPROVE WITH FIXES

**HIGH: a refused head combined with a hold, a pause or a spent token budget brings back the F370
pile-up, and nothing counts it.**
- D3 relies on the resume arm's re-briefing to "drive a pass that counts the refused head". That
  holds only when the pass actually reaches `trigger_agent_directly`.
- For a held agent, `_attempt_turn` returns at the hold check (`turn_scheduler.py:~388`) before
  counting anything. It does the same at the token-budget check and at "conversation is
  unavailable". B3's paused agents join `agents_held`, so they behave the same way.
- So a held or paused assignee whose head is refused (attempts > 0, reason set) is re-briefed on
  every firing. Each briefing sits behind the head, so the pair stays refused, and briefings
  accumulate for the whole hold.
- **Fix:** in the resume arm, treat refused as not-attending only when a pass can reach the head:
  `agent in running or attends(t, agent) or (agent in held_agents and has_turn(t, agent))`, plus a
  guard for an exhausted budget. Add a test: a held agent, a refused head and three firings produce
  no new entries.

mirroring the review arm. The sentence names the prerequisite, the checkout and the merge command.
Without this, F158's D2 produces an endless re-brief and abandon cycle.
