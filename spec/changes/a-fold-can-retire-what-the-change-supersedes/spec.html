A fold can retire what the change supersedes in the capability

change-specapprovedsketch

3 MUST·3 requirements·4 criteria·3 tasks

Summary

Folding a change can also remove capability requirements (with their criteria) and individual criteria that the change supersedes, in the same audited merge. Amending a criterion is retiring the old one and folding the new. The fold dialog lists the capability's requirements and criteria to retire.

Problem

F533: the fold appends and can replace a whole requirement, but cannot remove a requirement or touch an existing criterion. Restating spec-document-authority's ac136, which a-finished-change-is-folded-into-its-capability contradicts, took a second hand-built merge naming no change (a504858), and a change that narrows or retires behaviour has no fold path at all.

Scope

In scope

  • retire and retire_criteria on the fold request, applied in the same merge
  • The fold draft listing the capability's requirements and criteria
  • The fold dialog's retire section

Non-goals

  • A change document declaring what it retires in its own payload: the fold is where the operator reconciles a change with the capability
  • Retiring through an agent: folds stay operator-only
  • Editing an existing criterion's text in place: retire it and fold the new one

Requirements

FR-1# Requirement 1 of 3

MUST A fold MUST accept capability requirement keys to retire and capability criterion keys to retire, and write the capability without them in the same merge as the folded requirements: a retired requirement takes its criteria with it. A key the capability does not hold, or a requirement both replaced and retired, MUST be refused before anything is written.

WhyA change that narrows or supersedes behaviour must be able to say so in the corpus through the same audited step that adds what it brings.

FR-2# Requirement 2 of 3

MUST The fold draft MUST list the capability's current requirements (key and statement) and criteria (key, requirement and then), so the operator can choose what to retire.

WhyThe operator retires by key; the keys have to be in front of them.

FR-3# Requirement 3 of 3

MUST The fold dialog MUST offer the capability's requirements and criteria for retirement, filterable by text, and send the chosen keys with the fold.

WhyThe close-out stays something the operator does in the app.

Design

Tier 1, no migration. Retirement is applied to the draft's payload before the merge, so the merge's validation (a criterion naming a missing requirement is refused) and its audit row cover it. Retiring a requirement drops its criteria; retiring a criterion leaves its requirement. HAZARD: the UI bundle reaches :8000's app at its next reload. Rollback: revert.
