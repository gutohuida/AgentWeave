Slice step-journey (The spec is written one step at a time) of the roadmap A spec is written one step at a time, and a tester keeps it true (spec/changes/a-spec-is-written-one-step-at-a-time-roadmap/spec.html)

12 MUST·1 MAY·13 requirements·14 criteria·4 tasks

Summary

A change document records its journey step and size. The spec agent is briefed with only the current step's instructions, writes the step's result into the document, and asks the operator to continue here, continue in a fresh conversation, or stop; any conversation on the document resumes at the recorded step. Intake sizes the work (fix, small, large) and the size shapes the journey. The operator sees the journey on the phase bar and can move it or change the size. The operator's 'exploration is complete' step before proposing is retired.

Problem

Every exploring turn carries the whole exploring duty and open-document block, about 7-8k characters (hub/hub/api/v1/agents.py:1720-1761, 2165-2284), so a long exploration drops steps; the Hub cannot say where an exploration is, and a fresh conversation has to rediscover it. Nothing sizes the work, so a one-line fix is explored like a hazard. Acceptance is asked for only as given/when/then per requirement (spec_payload.py:93-102), with no how-to-check and no word on who can check it, so the drive-first habit never reaches the product.

Scope

In scope

  • spec_documents: journey step and size, by migration
  • Per-step duties replacing the exploring duty for change documents, and a step line naming the journey
  • An agent tool that records a step move and answers with what the step left empty
  • Size fix, small or large, recorded at intake with a reason, overridable by the operator
  • Acceptance criteria gain optional how_to_check and checked_by (agent or operator)
  • The phase bar shows the journey, moves it, and changes the size; the Exploration is complete control and its gate are removed
  • GET /agent-context accepts spec_document and returns the briefing a turn on that document receives

Non-goals

  • Project-defined steps or appended instructions (slice project-steps)
  • Approval warnings, including for skipped steps (slice approval-warnings); proposing keeps its other refusals
  • Roadmap documents: they keep today's duty
  • Dropping the explore_closed_at column (kept, unread, for rollback)

Requirements

FR-1# Requirement 1 of 13

MUST A change document MUST record its current journey step and its size (fix, small, large, or none yet); a new change document MUST start at intake with no size.

WhyThe document, not the conversation, carries where the work is.

FR-2# Requirement 2 of 13

MUST The journey MUST follow the size: none or large gives intake, requirements, acceptance, approach, tasks, delivery; small gives intake, requirements-and-acceptance (one step), tasks, delivery; fix gives intake, tasks, delivery.

WhyCeremony scales with the work; acceptance is never dropped except for a fix, whose one task carries its failing check.

FR-3# Requirement 3 of 13

MUST A turn on an exploring change document MUST be briefed with the current step's instructions and one line naming the journey's steps and the current one, and MUST NOT carry another step's instructions.

WhyInstruction adherence falls as instructions grow; each step gets only what it needs.

FR-4# Requirement 4 of 13

MUST Every step's instructions MUST say what the step writes into the document and MUST tell the agent, once that is written, to ask the operator through ask_user whether to continue here, continue in a fresh conversation, or stop, and to advance only on either continue.

WhyThe agent proposes advancing; the operator decides (2026-10-08).

FR-5# Requirement 5 of 13

MUST The agent MUST be able to move its document to the next step, or to a named step, through a tool that records the move with its run and answers with the outputs the left step still lacks; it MUST NOT refuse a move for a missing output.

WhyWarn, never gate; what was skipped is on record for the approval warnings.

FR-6# Requirement 6 of 13

MUST A turn in any conversation on the document, including a new one or another agent's, MUST be briefed from the document's recorded step and size.

WhyDrop it today, continue another day.

FR-7# Requirement 7 of 13

MUST The intake step's instructions MUST have the agent ask one question at a time through ask_user, offering choices where it can, and record the size with its reason before asking to advance; the size MUST be told to the operator.

WhySizing is announced so it can be overridden.

FR-8# Requirement 8 of 13

MUST The acceptance step's instructions MUST have the agent agree with the operator, for each MUST requirement, at least one criterion with how to check it and whether an agent or only the operator can, and name the acceptance drive; the tasks step's instructions MUST make the first task write that drive and record it failing.

WhyAcceptance is the high-attention step; the drive-first habit becomes the product's.

FR-9# Requirement 9 of 13

MAY An acceptance criterion MAY carry how_to_check (text) and checked_by (agent or operator); a document without them MUST stay valid.

WhyAdditive, so every existing document reads unchanged.

FR-10# Requirement 10 of 13

MUST The document's phase bar MUST show the journey's steps, the current one and the size, and MUST let the operator move to any step, back or forward, and change the size, each change taking effect from the next turn.

WhySteps are revisitable and the size is the operator's to override.

FR-11# Requirement 11 of 13

MUST Proposing a change document MUST NOT require the operator to have marked exploration complete; the Exploration is complete control and the explore_not_closed refusal MUST be removed.

WhyThe step pointer replaces the boolean; one fewer operator click.

FR-12# Requirement 12 of 13

MUST The migration MUST place every exploring change document at intake when it has no requirements and at requirements otherwise, and leave other phases and roadmaps with no step; a document with no size MUST be briefed as large.

WhyNo document loses its place, and the operator's live data on :8000 migrates safely.

FR-13# Requirement 13 of 13

MUST GET /agent-context MUST accept a spec_document and return the briefing a turn on that document receives.

WhyWhat an agent is told becomes observable, by the drive and by the operator.

Design

Tier 2: a migration on spec_documents, and the operator's :8000 runs this checkout, so its next restart runs this migration on live data. Rollback: the downgrade drops the two columns; explore_closed_at is kept and simply no longer read, so a downgraded Hub gates proposing as before. The drive runs on its own scratch Hub, never on :8000; :8010 is restarted only after the build.

D1 The step is a column, not conversation state: a fresh conversation, another day or another agent reads it. Rejected: a step held in a checkpoint (dies with the lineage). D2 The journey is derived from the size in code (one table), so slice project-steps can replace that table with the project's setting without a second migration. D3 The advance tool never refuses; it reports what the step left empty. Skipped outputs become slice approval-warnings' input. D4 Duties are short and each names a marker phrase, so a test can prove a briefing holds one step and not another. D5 Proposing keeps its other refusals (a requirement with no criterion, delivery incomplete); turning those into warnings is slice approval-warnings.
