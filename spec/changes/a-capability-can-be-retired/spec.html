A capability can be retired

change-specarchivedsketch

5 MUST·5 requirements·5 criteria·3 tasks

Summary

The operator can retire a capability that no longer describes the product: with a stated reason, and optionally naming the capability that absorbed it. A retired capability is archived: its requirements are retired, so they leave coverage and nothing can link to them; no merge or fold can write to it; and its file stays as the record of what it said.

Problem

F536: quiet-hours (a sample), trace-timeline (never built) and opencode-config (runner removed) describe nothing in the code, and two capabilities that overlap cannot be combined: DELETE refuses a capability (delete_capability), `current` has no transition, and a merge can only empty one, leaving an empty `current` capability in the map, the index and coverage.

Scope

In scope

  • A current -> archived edge for capability documents, operator-only, with a required reason and an optional absorbing capability
  • Retiring every requirement of a retired capability, and keeping them retired on any later reindex
  • Refusing a merge or fold into a retired capability
  • A Retire action on a capability in the app

Non-goals

  • Deleting a capability or its file: the file and its history stay
  • Un-retiring: there is no transition out of archived, as for changes
  • Moving the requirements to the absorbing capability: that is a separate, authored merge
  • Retiring change, roadmap or system-map documents: they have their own archive rules

Requirements

FR-1# Requirement 1 of 5

MUST The operator MUST be able to move a capability from current to archived, stating a reason and optionally naming the current capability that absorbed it; the move MUST be refused without a reason, when the named capability is not another current capability, when the actor is not the operator, and while a task that is neither approved nor rejected links one of its active requirements.

WhyA corpus that cannot lose a capability keeps describing features that do not exist. Open work against it must be decided first, or that work would serve requirements nobody holds any more.

FR-2# Requirement 2 of 5

MUST Every requirement of an archived capability MUST be retired when it is archived and MUST stay retired whenever the document is indexed again.

WhyCoverage, linking and evidence all read the requirement index; a retired capability's requirements must leave all three at once, and a reindex from the unchanged file must not bring them back.

FR-3# Requirement 3 of 5

MUST A merge or fold into an archived capability MUST be refused, writing nothing.

WhyThe file is the record of what the capability said when it was retired.

FR-4# Requirement 4 of 5

MUST Retiring MUST record a phase event carrying the reason and the absorbing capability, re-render the file with its archived status, and keep the document in spec/index.json with status archived.

WhyWhy a capability left, and where its behaviour went, must be readable later from the document's own history.

FR-5# Requirement 5 of 5

MUST A current capability's view in the app MUST offer Retire, asking for the reason and optionally the absorbing capability, and MUST show the Hub's refusal when one is returned.

WhyRetiring is an operator decision made where the operator reads the capability.

Design

Tier 2: a migration. HAZARD: 0121 recreates spec_documents to relax ck_spec_documents_kind_phase to (capability AND phase IN (current, archived)) OR (not capability AND phase != current); it reaches the operator's real :8000 database at their next restart, and the UI bundle reaches :8000's app at its next reload. Nothing existing changes value. Rollback: downgrade sets any archived capability back to current, then restores the narrow CHECK (retirements are lost; requirement rows stay retired until the next reindex). Decision (operator 2026-10-08: 'Build retire a capability'): reuse archived rather than a new phase, so the vocabulary and the index's statuses do not grow; the reason and absorbing capability live on the phase event, not a column. Requirements are retired by one rule in the index (archived capability => retired), not by editing the file, so the file stays the record. Folds into spec-document-authority.
