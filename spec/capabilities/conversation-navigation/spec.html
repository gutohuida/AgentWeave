Conversation navigation

capabilitycurrentsketch

13 MUST·13 requirements·45 criteria

Summary

How the operator finds and opens conversations: the project/agent/conversation tree in the rail, the recency view, row attention state and menus, URL-backed navigation, and the new-conversation surface.

Scope

In scope

  • The rail's project/agent/conversation tree and the recency view across agents
  • Per-row attention state and row action menus
  • URL-backed, project-scoped conversation navigation
  • Starting a new conversation from navigation; grouping loop firings and archived agents' conversations

Non-goals

  • What an open conversation shows (agent-conversation-workspace)
  • The rail's shell, theming and project header (hub-workspace-shell)
  • The composer (agent-composer)

Requirements

FR-1# Requirement 1 of 13

SHALL Selecting an agent from navigation or from the project overview's agent roster SHALL open that agent's conversation directly, with no intermediate agent list, filter tab bar or selection placeholder.

WhyReading the conversation is the reason for selecting the agent; any intermediate step is a click that carries no information.

FR-2# Requirement 2 of 13

SHALL Navigation SHALL list every registered project as a named entry whose children are its agents, and each agent as an entry whose children are its conversations; a project's or agent's name and its expander SHALL be separately activatable, the name navigating and the expander toggling without navigating.

WhyExpanding to look and opening to go are different intents, and one control cannot express both.

FR-3# Requirement 3 of 13

SHALL From an open agent conversation, the containing project's overview SHALL be reachable in a single action.

WhyThe conversation header carries a back-to-project control so the operator never has to find the project in navigation to leave a conversation.

FR-4# Requirement 4 of 13

MUST Navigation MUST NOT offer an *Agents* destination or a *Messages* destination; the agent and message records and their APIs remain unchanged.

WhyAgents are listed in the tree and peer traffic is merged into each conversation's timeline, so separate destinations would only duplicate them.

FR-5# Requirement 5 of 13

SHALL Navigation SHALL read its project and agent tree from the collection of every registered project, SHALL let the operator open an existing directory, create a new project and switch projects without changing credentials, and MUST NOT leak conversations, drafts, cached server state or in-flight results across projects.

WhyOne local Hub owns many projects; every piece of client state is keyed by project id.

FR-6# Requirement 6 of 13

MUST Project, agent, conversation and settings navigation SHALL be represented in the browser URL using project id and AgentWeave conversation id, and reload and back/forward SHALL restore the represented destination; provider session identifiers MUST NOT be used as URL identity.

WhyA destination the operator cannot reload or link to is not a place they can return to.

FR-7# Requirement 7 of 13

SHALL Navigation SHALL list an agent's open conversations beneath it, most recent activity first, each labelled by its title and marked when a peer started it; activating the agent's name SHALL open its most recent conversation, and beyond 7 conversations the rest SHALL sit behind an expander that states how many are hidden.

WhyA silent cut-off reads as data loss; an unbounded list buries the agent rows below it.

FR-8# Requirement 8 of 13

SHALL Navigation SHALL offer a recency view listing the project's open conversations across all agents, most recent first, each with its agent's identity colour as a leading edge; the agent tree SHALL be the default, the chosen view SHALL persist across reloads, archived conversations SHALL be reachable through a control stating their count, and beyond 15 conversations the rest SHALL sit behind an expander stating how many are hidden.

WhyThe tree costs a level of hierarchy; the recency view recovers the flat scan without losing which agent owns each thread.

FR-9# Requirement 9 of 13

SHALL Navigation SHALL show, for each listed conversation, whether it is running, waiting on the operator, or idle — waiting meaning it holds an unanswered question still being waited on or a pending permission request — with waiting distinguishable from and outranking running, and a collapsed agent SHALL show when any of its conversations is waiting.

WhyA waiting run consumes its timeout while the operator is unaware; once the wait has ended there is nothing left to surface.

FR-10# Requirement 10 of 13

MUST Every agent row and conversation row in navigation SHALL expose its actions through a menu opened from a control on the row, reachable by keyboard, shown at rest on the active row and revealed on hover or focus elsewhere; a conversation row SHALL offer rename and archive (unarchive when archived), and an agent row SHALL offer new conversation, agent settings and the archived-conversation listing, with an unavailable item shown disabled with its reason.

WhyAgent settings is a linkable destination with a fixed way back to the agent's most recent conversation, not a dialog.

FR-11# Requirement 11 of 13

SHALL Starting a new conversation SHALL be initiated from navigation and SHALL open a surface whose composer is the primary element, led by a question naming the bound agent (or asking which agent should take the work); the operator SHALL be able to retarget to any project agent, the recency view's start SHALL require choosing an agent before sending, and no conversation record SHALL be created until the first message is sent.

WhyAn abandoned start leaves nothing behind, and a pre-selected agent is a default, not a binding.

FR-12# Requirement 12 of 13

SHALL Navigation SHALL group an agent's consecutive conversations from the same loop's firings into one expandable row, and the display cap SHALL never split such a group.

WhyA loop firing every few minutes would otherwise fill an agent's list with near-identical rows.

FR-13# Requirement 13 of 13

SHALL Open conversations belonging to an archived agent SHALL be listed in navigation in a marked group of their own, and opening one SHALL offer to unarchive the agent.

WhyThe tree iterates the open roster, so these conversations otherwise vanished while the recency view still listed them.

Design

D1. Navigation is its own capability. agent-conversation-workspace had grown to 84 requirements; finding a conversation and reading one change for different reasons and are tested in different places (rail tree vs transcript), so they are specified apart.

D2. Rail chrome stays in hub-workspace-shell; this capability owns what the navigation lists and how a conversation is reached from it.

Lifecycle

Split out of agent-conversation-workspace in the 2026-10-08 spec overhaul.
