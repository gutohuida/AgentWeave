Usage accounting

Summary

Every Hub-owned run gets one durable token-accounting outcome, measured or explicitly unavailable; usage is aggregated per agent, conversation and project; allowance and cost are presented without implying a bill; Copilot's AI credits are recorded beside tokens; and a project token budget pauses autonomous work while the operator keeps control.

Problem

Runners report usage in different shapes and sometimes not at all. Without one honest record per run, missing data reads as zero, estimates read as charges, and a budget either stops nothing or stops the operator too.

Scope

In scope

  • `TurnUsage` rows and their normalisation from Claude, Codex and Copilot telemetry
  • `GET /accounting`, `GET /accounting/conversations/{id}`, `PATCH /accounting/budget`
  • Copilot AI credits and premium requests, for runs and one-shot calls
  • The token budget gate for autonomous turns, and how accounting is shown

Non-goals

  • The context-window meter (agent-context-usage)
  • Hop budget and delivery cap (turn scheduling)

Requirements

FR-7# Requirement 1 of 14

MUST The Hub SHALL persist exactly one accounting outcome per Hub-owned run after it ends: `measured`, with normalised input, output and total tokens and cache and reasoning breakdowns where reported, or `unavailable`, which MUST NOT be represented as zero.

WhyA missing figure shown as zero understates usage and cannot be told apart from a free turn.

FR-8# Requirement 2 of 14

SHALL The Hub SHALL normalise Claude result usage, Codex completed-turn usage and Copilot usage into one accounting shape without double-counting cache or reasoning subsets, and malformed telemetry SHALL yield an `unavailable` outcome rather than failing the run.

WhyOne shape makes per-agent and per-project totals comparable across runners.

FR-15# Requirement 3 of 14

SHALL A Copilot run's tokens SHALL be the larger of the sum of its per-call usage (each call once, including subagent and compaction calls) and its process's latest cumulative prompt usage for this run, never their sum, and cache reads SHALL NOT be added to its input or total again.

WhyEach Copilot figure is a lower bound; Copilot's input already includes cache reads.

FR-9# Requirement 4 of 14

MUST `GET /accounting` SHALL report measured token totals per agent and per project, with measured and unavailable turn counts and the most recent turns, and SHALL NOT invent values for unmeasured turns.

WhyThe operator needs to see who used what, and how much of it is unknown.

FR-16# Requirement 5 of 14

SHALL `GET /accounting/conversations/{id}` SHALL aggregate every run of that conversation, uncapped, and SHALL answer 404 for a conversation that does not exist or belongs to another project.

WhyAn aggregate over no rows is a valid zero, so an unknown conversation must not read as one that cost nothing (F239).

FR-10# Requirement 6 of 14

MUST Accounting SHALL prefer the latest runner-reported rate-limit allowance (naming its runner) over any monetary figure; otherwise a runner-reported monetary figure SHALL be labelled "API-equivalent estimate" and state how many turns reported no cost, and the Hub MUST NOT compute money from a price catalogue.

WhySubscription users are not billed per token; an estimate that silently omits turns overstates its coverage.

FR-11# Requirement 7 of 14

SHALL An operator SHALL be able to set a positive project token budget or clear it; while measured project usage is at or above it, autonomous turns SHALL stay queued with the waiting reason "token budget exhausted", and operator-initiated turns SHALL still start and be accounted.

WhyA budget exists to stop runaway autonomy, not to lock the operator out of their own project.

FR-17# Requirement 8 of 14

SHALL A budget change SHALL be recorded as an `accounting_budget_updated` event and SHALL reconsider every agent with queued entries, so work held by an exhausted budget starts once the budget allows it.

WhyRaising the budget should release what it was holding without another nudge.

FR-12# Requirement 9 of 14

SHALL The interface SHALL show the project's measured token total and budget, SHALL show unavailable usage as unavailable rather than zero, and when the budget is exhausted SHALL state that autonomous turns are paused and operator messages can still run.

WhyA paused project that looks broken sends the operator looking for the wrong fault.

FR-13# Requirement 10 of 14

SHALL The Hub SHALL record the AI credits and premium requests each Copilot run reports and show them beside tokens on the turn, conversation, agent and project, and accounting responses SHALL carry these fields for every project, null where none were reported.

WhyCredits are what Copilot meters; a project with no Copilot runs must look as it did before credits existed.

FR-18# Requirement 11 of 14

SHALL A Copilot run's credits SHALL be the larger of its session total minus the total the previous run of that session recorded (zero for a new session) and the sum of its own per-call credit costs; when the difference cannot be taken, is negative, or is smaller than that sum, the run SHALL be charged the sum with premium requests unknown, and each run SHALL record the session total it reached, the previous run being the one written last.

WhyCopilot reports a session-wide running total; no credit may be charged to two runs, and no run charged negative credits, even if the clock steps backwards.

FR-19# Requirement 12 of 14

SHALL A negative, non-numeric, boolean, non-finite or larger-than-2^53-1 credit or premium-request figure SHALL be ignored and that figure recorded as unknown, without failing the run or losing its tokens.

WhyA bad figure from the provider should cost one field, not the run's record.

FR-20# Requirement 13 of 14

SHALL AI credits SHALL be stored in Copilot's own unit and shown as AI credits, and SHALL NOT be converted to money, added to any token total, or counted toward a token budget or its exhaustion.

WhyCredits are the provider's report of consumption; the budget is denominated in tokens only.

FR-14# Requirement 14 of 14

SHALL A Copilot one-shot call (checkpoint, probe, or other Hub-owned `copilot -p` call with an invocation record) that exits successfully SHALL record the credits and premium requests of the last session usage checkpoint in its output, applying the same malformed-figure rule per figure; with no checkpoint, or when the process failed, timed out or did not start, both SHALL be unknown, never zero.

WhyA one-shot call opens one session, so its last cumulative checkpoint is its whole charge; checkpoints must not be summed.

Design

D1 Rows are immutable facts; every total is derived from `TurnUsage` by aggregate query (`hub/hub/usage_accounting.py`), so a fix to presentation never rewrites history.

D2 Unavailable is a status, not a zero: aggregates sum only `measured` rows and report the unavailable count beside them.

D3 Presentation preference is allowance, then API-equivalent estimate (with unpriced-turn count), then tokens, then unavailable. No price catalogue is consulted.

D4 Credits are stored as `ai_nano_aiu` (Copilot's unit) and `premium_requests`, nullable, separate from tokens, and never read by the budget gate (`project_budget_state` sums `total_tokens` only).

D5 The budget gate lives in `turn_scheduler` and applies only to turns whose initiator is `autonomous`.
