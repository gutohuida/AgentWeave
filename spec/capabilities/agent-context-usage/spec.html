Agent context usage

capabilitycurrentsketch

HomeAgents and execution

18 MUST·18 requirements·28 criteria

Summary

How full an agent's context window is, measured from what each runner reports, expressed as one canonical sample per reading, and shown on every surface the same way, including the open conversation's header. A reading replaces the previous one and is never summed. It names the model it measured. It is expressed against a window the provider reported or the catalog declares for that model, and is reported as unknown otherwise. Readings are stored as `context_warning` events, attributed to a conversation, broadcast to open clients, and kept apart from the run's output stream.

Problem

Context pressure that is summed across calls, measured against a window that belongs to a different model, or shown as 0% when nothing was measured misleads the operator and the checkpoint policy alike.

Scope

In scope

  • Mapping each runner's usage reports to a context sample (`runner_parsing.py`, `codex_appserver.py`, `copilot_acp.py`).
  • Recording, choosing and projecting readings (`output_recording.record_context_usage`, `context_readings.usable_context_reading`, `POST /agents/{name}/context-usage`).
  • Presenting a reading in the app (`hub/ui/src/components/context/contextPresentation.ts`), including the conversation header's indicator for the open conversation.

Non-goals

  • Token and cost accounting per turn: usage-accounting.
  • Which window the catalog declares for each model: model-catalog.
  • When a reading triggers a checkpoint: conversation-checkpoint.
  • The header's other controls: agent-conversation-workspace.

Requirements

FR-19# Requirement 1 of 18

SHALL A context reading SHALL be one sample with `status` (`measured`, `estimated`, `unsupported`, `unavailable`), `source`, `basis` (`provider_context`, `latest_request_input`, `provider_reported_ratio`, `cumulative_delta`) for a measured or estimated sample, optional non-negative `context_tokens`, optional positive `limit_tokens`, `percent` derived from tokens and limit (or reported directly as a ratio), optional `model` and `session_id`, `observed_at`, and an optional numeric breakdown limited to known keys.

WhyOne shape from runner to screen prevents field-name drift, and a percentage derived from its operands cannot contradict them.

FR-20# Requirement 2 of 18

SHALL A reading SHALL describe the latest non-cumulative observation, and a later reading SHALL replace an earlier one; readings from separate calls, steps or turns SHALL NOT be summed.

WhyEach request re-sends the conversation, so summing requests counts the same context many times.

FR-21# Requirement 3 of 18

SHALL Cache token fields SHALL be added to context tokens only where the provider reports them as exclusive of input (Claude), and SHALL be kept as breakdown only where the provider's input already includes them (Codex, Copilot).

WhyAdding an inclusive cache figure double-counts it.

FR-22# Requirement 4 of 18

SHALL For Claude, each assistant message's usage SHALL yield a measured `latest_request_input` sample of `input_tokens + cache_read_input_tokens + cache_creation_input_tokens` naming the message's model, and the end-of-turn `result` SHALL yield the window from `modelUsage.<model>.contextWindow` of the model with the largest window.

WhyClaude reports tokens per request and the window only at the end of the turn; the primary model is the one with the largest window, not an auxiliary one used by subagents.

FR-23# Requirement 5 of 18

SHALL For Codex over its app-server transport, each `thread/tokenUsage/updated` SHALL yield a sample of `last.inputTokens + last.outputTokens` against `modelContextWindow`, measured with basis `provider_context`, or unavailable when no window is reported; over the exec transport, `turn.completed.usage` SHALL yield an estimated `cumulative_delta` sample against the catalog's window, or unavailable when the catalog declares none.

WhyApp-server reports the per-request figure and its window itself; exec reports figures that can be cumulative across a resumed session, so they are labelled estimated rather than presented as a measurement.

FR-37# Requirement 6 of 18

SHALL For Copilot, the latest `usage_update` of a turn SHALL yield a measured `provider_context` sample with context tokens `used` and limit `size`, naming the model Copilot reports it ran (else the requested model); an update with no positive `size` SHALL yield an unavailable sample carrying `used` and SHALL NOT borrow a window from the catalog or another model; the prompt response's `usage` SHALL NOT be used as a context reading.

WhyCopilot reports the window of the model it actually ran (which under Auto may differ from any catalog entry); its prompt-level usage is cumulative for the session.

FR-31# Requirement 7 of 18

SHALL The window a reading is expressed against SHALL be, in order: the window the provider reported for that reading; else the catalog's window for the reading's model (exact id, then alias, then the longest declared id the reported one starts with); else unknown, never a default that does not describe the model.

WhyProviders report dated snapshot ids the catalog may hold undated; a window right for the model family beats none, and a default for a different model is worse than none.

FR-34# Requirement 8 of 18

SHALL A measured sample SHALL carry the model that ran the turn it measured.

WhyThe catalog is keyed by model; an unidentified sample can only resolve to unknown, which is how Claude agents once reported no percentage in 329 samples.

FR-32# Requirement 9 of 18

SHALL When no window can be resolved, the reading SHALL carry no limit and no percentage, the app SHALL show the token count (if any) without a bar, and no context-pressure condition (warning, critical, or checkpoint threshold) SHALL be raised from it.

WhyA percentage of an unresolved window is invented, and a checkpoint or pause triggered by it acts on a number nobody measured.

FR-33# Requirement 10 of 18

SHALL Each reading SHALL be expressed against the window of the model that ran its turn, so a conversation whose model changed reports pressure for the model now in use.

WhyTurns in one conversation may run on models with different windows.

FR-35# Requirement 11 of 18

SHALL When the newest reading for an agent or conversation carries no percentage, the Hub SHALL report the newest reading from the same provider session that does, and SHALL NOT fall back across sessions.

WhyA runner may report tokens and window in separate messages, so the last message can carry no percentage; a reset or new session makes an older session's percentage untrue.

FR-25# Requirement 12 of 18

SHALL A reading SHALL be attributed to the conversation whose provider session it names (else the agent's latest open conversation), and a reading whose `observed_at` is not newer than the agent's latest stored reading SHALL be ignored.

WhyA late reading from an earlier invocation must not overwrite the current one, and a new conversation starts with no reading rather than its predecessor's.

FR-26# Requirement 13 of 18

SHALL Each reading SHALL be stored as a `context_warning` event, broadcast over SSE as `context_warning`, and projected into `AgentSummary.context_usage` and each conversation's context reading, carrying the same fields; `POST /agents/{name}/context-usage` SHALL validate a submitted sample (enums, ranges, breakdown keys, percent consistency) and refuse an invalid one.

WhyEvery surface reads one stored record, so none can disagree; an unvalidated dictionary would let any shape reach the screen.

FR-38# Requirement 14 of 18

SHALL A reading identical to the agent's latest stored reading except for a newer `observed_at` SHALL NOT be stored or broadcast again, while still counting as fresh for the checkpoint policy.

WhyRepeated samples of an unchanged measurement once made two thirds of an activity log; readings differing only in model are not identical.

FR-27# Requirement 15 of 18

SHALL Readers SHALL normalize unambiguous legacy fields (`tokens_used`, `tokens_limit`, `input_tokens`, `context_limit`, ratio `context_usage`) into the canonical sample, and contradictory or incomplete legacy values SHALL become unavailable or token-only rather than a zero percentage.

WhyOlder stored rows and clients wrote a 0% on every reset; trusting it paints a bar for an unmeasured session.

FR-28# Requirement 16 of 18

SHALL Every surface that shows context SHALL use one normalizer and render measured, estimated, token-only, unavailable and unsupported states consistently, with warning at 70% and critical at 90% for measured readings only, and estimated readings labelled as estimated.

WhyThe same reading must look the same in the status bar, the rail and the conversation, and an estimate must not alarm like a measurement.

FR-29# Requirement 17 of 18

SHALL Context readings SHALL be delivered as replaceable snapshots and SHALL NOT be stored as `AgentOutput`, given a stream event kind, or rendered by the stream-event renderer.

WhyA reading supersedes the last one; a stream event is a permanent entry in the run's record.

FR-39# Requirement 18 of 18

SHALL The conversation header SHALL display the open conversation's own most recent context-usage reading, and SHALL render no indicator, rather than a zero, where no reading exists.

WhyA per-agent reading showed whichever thread last reported; a zero would assert a measurement that was never taken.

Behaviour

Recording a context reading

  1. If the sample has no limit and no percent but has tokens and a model, fill the limit from the catalog (`context_window_for_model`) and derive the percent.
  2. Attribute it to the conversation whose provider session it names, else the agent's latest open conversation.
  3. If its `observed_at` is not newer than the agent's latest stored reading, ignore it.
  4. If it equals the latest stored reading apart from `observed_at`, do not store or broadcast it.
  5. Otherwise store it as a `context_warning` event and broadcast it.
  6. Pass it to the checkpoint policy.

Design

**Snapshots, not stream events.** Readings live in `EventLog` as `context_warning` rows and are chosen per agent and per conversation by `usable_context_reading`, the single place that decides which reading is current.

**Complete the reading where it lands.** Claude reports tokens and window in different messages; rather than waiting for them to meet, `resolve_usage_limit` completes a sample from the catalog by its model at the one point every write path passes through.

**Provider report first.** Codex app-server and Copilot report their own window; Claude reports it at turn end; Codex exec reports none, so its readings are catalog-based and labelled estimated.

**Codex remains mapped** even though Codex is no longer driven; both of its transports are still built.

Evidence and coverage limits

Checked

  • hub/tests/test_context_usage.py, test_context_usage_measurement.py, test_conversation_context_usage.py — mapping, window resolution, recording, projection.
  • hub/tests/test_runner_parsing.py — Claude and Codex exec parsing.
  • hub/tests/test_copilot_acp_run_turn.py — Copilot `usage_update`.
  • hub/ui/src/__tests__/contextPresentation.test.tsx — presentation states.

Limits

  • `resolve_usage_limit` fills a catalog window into any sample with tokens and a model, including one whose status is `unavailable`: a Copilot `usage_update` with no `size` on `claude-sonnet-5` is stored with a 1,000,000 limit and a percent (run 2026-10-08), against `copilot-context-mapping`, and the checkpoint trigger reads `percent` without checking `status`.
  • Hub-internal samples are not validated by `ContextUsageCreate`; an unavailable sample carrying `context_tokens`, which the Hub records for Copilot and Codex, would be refused by the POST route.
  • `POST /agents/{name}/context-usage` has no caller in the repository outside tests.
