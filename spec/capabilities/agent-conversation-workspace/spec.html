Agent conversation workspace

Summary

The agent conversation is the operator's primary workspace, and AgentWeave owns a durable conversation id that outlives every provider session beneath it. This capability covers the conversation surface itself: the header's turn-level controls with the always-visible checkpoint control, how continuity is stated, how the timeline is laid out, folded and followed as output arrives, the banner stack of standing conditions, the runtime overrides a conversation remembers, and staying legible at every width.

Scope

In scope

  • Durable conversation identity, and stating continuity without exposing provider session ids.
  • Header controls at rest, including the checkpoint control.
  • Timeline presentation: one continuous plane, the reference measure, neutral operator messages, turn folding, disclosure hover, folded outbound peer messages.
  • Following output: opening at the newest entry, following by scroll position, Jump to newest, placing a new turn at the top.
  • The banner stack above the composer.
  • Runtime overrides a conversation remembers.
  • Legibility of the surface at every width it is offered.

Non-goals

  • The navigation tree, recency view, conversation URLs and starting a conversation: conversation-navigation.
  • Composer behaviour (drafts, sizing, queueing while running, routing statement, model and runtime controls): agent-composer.
  • The context-usage reading shown in the header: agent-context-usage.
  • Markdown and tool-call rendering of entries: agent-stream-events.
  • Peer-message routing into recipient conversations: peer-message-routing.
  • Delivery of queued input, hop budget, retries and refusal reporting: inbound-queue-delivery.
  • Holding an agent whose provider allowance is spent: provider-allowance-holds.
  • Review-turn checkouts and agent workspaces: workspace-isolation.
  • Titles, origin, rename and archival: conversation-lifecycle.
  • Checkpoint generation and cutover: conversation-checkpoint, agent-conversation-handoff.

Requirements

FR-48# Requirement 1 of 18

SHALL The system SHALL allocate a stable `conversation_id` before starting a provider process. A conversation SHALL belong to exactly one project and one immutable target agent, SHALL remain the same across runs, retries, stops, failures and provider-session binding, and SHALL treat the provider session as nullable continuation data rather than as its identity.

WhyRuns, queue entries and recorded output all carry the conversation id, so the operator-facing thread survives everything that happens to the provider process beneath it.

FR-56# Requirement 2 of 18

SHALL The conversation header SHALL expose, at rest, the conversation's context usage, the checkpoint (handoff) control, fold-all, agent settings and the side-panel toggle, plus a stop control while the agent is running; the composer SHALL expose only the model and runtime controls, the specification control where a document panel exists, and send. There SHALL be no conversation overflow menu.

WhyTurn-level controls sit beside the turn's state; conversation switching and starting live in navigation, so a menu that duplicated them was removed.

FR-57# Requirement 3 of 18

SHALL The current conversation's continuity SHALL be shown on the resting surface as readable text naming the conversation by its title (or stating that the next message starts a new conversation); provider session identifiers MUST NOT appear in normal navigation, URLs, drafts or labels and MAY appear only in details or diagnostic surfaces.

WhyThe operator works in AgentWeave conversations; the provider session is an implementation detail that changes underneath them.

FR-58# Requirement 4 of 18

SHALL Following of new output SHALL be determined by scroll position alone: pinned while the view is within 40px of the bottom, suspended once the operator scrolls away, and resumed when they return; there SHALL be no manual pause/resume control.

WhyThe scroll position already expresses the operator's intent.

FR-59# Requirement 5 of 18

SHALL A banner region directly above the composer SHALL display the conversation's standing conditions — run or submission failure, live-update stream loss, a hop-budget-blocked queue, and the conversation's task binding and checkpoint offers — stacking simultaneous conditions in a fixed order, each stating its own condition.

WhyA fixed order means a cleared condition never reshuffles the ones that remain.

FR-65# Requirement 6 of 18

SHALL A conversation SHALL retain the runtime overrides last submitted for it and run subsequent turns under them; a conversation the operator starts SHALL begin with none, while a conversation opened by a peer message or a job SHALL start from a copy of the agent's most recent overrides, never carrying `bypassPermissions` and, for a job, never `manual`.

WhyOverrides are per conversation so changing one thread never alters another or the agent's runner; copying into peer- and job-opened threads keeps a delegated turn in the posture the operator chose.

FR-68# Requirement 7 of 18

MUST A turn SHALL render expanded unless the operator has folded it, every turn (including the newest) SHALL be foldable, and appending a turn MUST NOT change the folded state of any existing turn.

WhyFolding derived from position collapsed the turn the operator was reading the moment a new one arrived.

FR-69# Requirement 8 of 18

MUST An operator message SHALL be distinguished from an agent message by placement and a neutral surface and border, and MUST NOT be tinted with the interface's accent colour.

WhyThe accent is reserved for focus and selection state.

FR-71# Requirement 9 of 18

SHALL Opening or switching to a conversation SHALL place the view at its newest entry, once per arrival, and SHALL resume following new output.

WhyThe most recent activity is what an operator opening a conversation is looking for; landing again on every streamed update would fight the operator's scrolling.

FR-72# Requirement 10 of 18

SHALL Whether the view follows new output SHALL be determined by the conversation entries actually displayed, not by any other stream.

WhyFollowing that observes a different source than the one rendered either fails to move or moves when nothing visible changed.

FR-73# Requirement 11 of 18

SHALL While following is suspended, and only then, the conversation SHALL offer a "Jump to newest" control that returns the view to the newest entry and resumes following.

WhyIt expresses "take me back", not a toggle; following stays governed by scroll position.

FR-79# Requirement 12 of 18

MUST The checkpoint (durable handoff) control SHALL be present on the open conversation's header, labelled and visible at rest, MUST NOT be behind a menu or a navigation row action, and SHALL be shown disabled with its reason when unavailable.

WhyAn operator who cannot see the handoff forgets it exists; a disabled control keeps the header's shape stable across agents.

FR-86# Requirement 13 of 18

MUST The conversation SHALL read as one continuous surface: its header SHALL sit on the ground plane and separate itself from scrolled content by translucency and blur rather than a fill and dividing rule, and the region holding the composer MUST NOT be drawn as a filled band.

WhyStacked filled bands made the conversation read as boxes inside boxes.

FR-89# Requirement 14 of 18

SHALL Conversation entries SHALL be laid out in a centred column at most 960px wide with 30px side gutters and 21px between entries, and the composer region SHALL be centred at most 820px wide.

WhyA bounded measure keeps lines readable on wide screens.

FR-90# Requirement 15 of 18

SHALL The work disclosure's summary and the folded-turn control SHALL take a hover treatment; the work disclosure itself SHALL carry no border or fill, so it stays subordinate to the message it belongs to.

WhyA boxed, filled work block drew more attention than the agent's own answer.

FR-97# Requirement 16 of 18

SHALL An outbound peer message SHALL render folded by default on one line showing its recipient and its subject (falling back to its first line), SHALL expand to its full content on request, and SHALL stay expanded as the conversation grows; inbound peer messages render with their content visible.

WhyThe send is already announced as a tool row; showing the subject keeps several messages to one recipient distinguishable.

FR-120# Requirement 17 of 18

SHALL While the view is following, a newly appended turn SHALL be brought to the top of the viewport, with blank tail space below it that shrinks as the turn grows, so following becomes ordinary bottom-following once the turn fills the viewport.

WhyThe start of a long response is what the operator reads first; pinning only the bottom scrolled it away as it streamed.

FR-136# Requirement 18 of 18

SHALL Every control the conversation surface presents SHALL stay within its container at every width the surface can be shown at, wrapping rather than being removed or clipped, and no two interactive elements SHALL overlap.

WhyThe surface is shown beside a document in a pane narrower than a full-width conversation; the permission control in particular must remain readable before sending. The check is geometric, not presence in the DOM.

Design

D1 Conversation id is AgentWeave's; the provider session is nullable continuation data. A run that reports a different provider session fails with a binding conflict and does not return its input, because the turn already ran.

D2 The header holds turn-level controls only. Switching, starting and configuring belong to navigation; the header keeps context usage, the always-visible Checkpoint control, fold-all, settings, the side-panel toggle and stop while running. There is no overflow menu.

D3 Following is scroll-position driven (40px threshold), lands once per conversation arrival, and offers "Jump to newest" only while suspended.
