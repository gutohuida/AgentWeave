Summary

A conversation is a durable Hub record, independent of the provider session beneath it. It carries a readable title (truncated from its first message, optionally upgraded by a model, never overwriting a title the operator set) and an immutable origin. It can be renamed, archived and unarchived; archiving is reversible tidying, refused while work is outstanding, and a sender addressing an archived conversation is told what to do instead and handed back its message.

Problem

Operators navigate many conversations per agent. Identifiers are unreadable as labels, a conversation's provenance matters when reasoning about posture and delivery, and tidying the list must never strand queued work or stop a run.

Scope

In scope

  • Conversation titles: truncation, operator rename, optional model generation
  • Conversation origin
  • Archive and unarchive, and their refusals
  • Refusing sends and triggers addressed to an archived conversation

Non-goals

  • Navigation of conversations in the rail (agent-conversation-workspace)
  • Archiving as part of a handoff cutover (agent-conversation-handoff)
  • Runtime override inheritance between conversations (agent-conversation-workspace)

Requirements

FR-8# Requirement 1 of 9

MUST A conversation SHALL be titled from the text of the first message queued into it, whitespace collapsed and cut at a word boundary within 120 characters (a single longer word is cut at the limit); later messages MUST NOT retitle it. A conversation that predates titling SHALL be titled from its first message when it is next listed. No operator surface SHALL show a conversation's identifier as its label; a conversation with no title is shown as new.

WhyNavigation must be readable without knowing ids; the first message is the best cheap description of a thread.

FR-9# Requirement 2 of 9

SHALL Every conversation SHALL record an immutable `origin` at creation from the set `operator`, `peer`, `handoff`, `spec`, `job`, `divergence`: `operator` when the operator's trigger, an operator-sent message or an operator answer opens it, `peer` when a peer agent's message or agent request opens it for the recipient, `handoff` for a cutover successor, `job` for a scheduled firing, and `divergence` when a diverged run's work is escalated or restaffed to a different agent. Creating a conversation without an origin SHALL be an error.

WhyProvenance can only be known where a conversation is created; a default would record every peer and job conversation as the operator's, and posture inheritance depends on it.

FR-10# Requirement 3 of 9

MUST `PATCH /api/v1/projects/{project_id}/agents/{agent}/conversations/{conversation_id}` SHALL set the title (whitespace collapsed) and record it as operator-set, after which no generated title SHALL replace it; an empty title or one over 120 characters SHALL be refused with 400 and a stated reason, and an unknown conversation with 404. The rail's row menu SHALL offer Rename inline, treating an empty or unchanged entry as a cancel.

WhyAn operator's own name for a thread is a decision, not a draft for the titler to improve.

FR-11# Requirement 4 of 9

SHALL A project SHALL carry `conversation_title_mode` (`truncate`, the default, or `generate`) and an optional `conversation_title_runner_id` from the same project, both editable in the project settings panel. Under `generate`, after each completed turn the Hub SHALL run a one-shot titling command, in the project's own directory, on the chosen runner or else the agent's own runner, from the opening message and first reply; it SHALL replace the title only when the operator has not set one (re-checked after generation), SHALL skip the call when the same excerpt already produced the current title, and SHALL record a `conversation_titled` event rather than a run.

WhyTruncation is the free floor; generation is opt-in. Recording a run under the agent's name would make the agent look busy and stall its queue, and the excerpt check stops every later turn paying for the same title.

FR-15# Requirement 5 of 9

MUST A titling run MUST NOT be bound to any conversation, resume any provider session, appear in any timeline or change any context usage; when it fails, times out (45 s), has no usable runner, or the project directory cannot be resolved, the truncated title SHALL stay and the agent's run SHALL be unaffected.

WhyA title is never worth failing or delaying a completed turn.

FR-12# Requirement 6 of 9

SHALL The operator SHALL be able to archive a conversation (`POST .../conversations/{id}/archive`), setting its lifecycle to `archived` and recording the time, and to unarchive it (`POST .../unarchive`), returning it to `open` and clearing that time. Archiving an archived conversation SHALL succeed as a no-op, and unarchiving SHALL never be refused. Conversation listings SHALL exclude archived conversations by default, return exactly them with `lifecycle=archived`, and the project listing SHALL report archived counts in total and per agent. An archived conversation SHALL remain fully readable.

WhyArchiving is reversible tidying, never deletion; the counts let navigation offer "show archived" without a second request.

FR-13# Requirement 7 of 9

MUST Archiving SHALL be refused with 409 and a sentence naming the obstruction when the conversation has a running run or an undelivered queued entry, and the system MUST NOT stop a run or discard a queued entry to satisfy the request.

WhyNothing delivers into an archived conversation, so archiving would strand queued work, and stopping a live run from a row menu destroys work with no undo.

FR-14# Requirement 8 of 9

MUST A message sent to an archived recipient conversation SHALL be refused with 409 stating that the conversation is archived, instructing the sender to omit `conversation_id` to reach a new conversation, and restating the submitted content; no message or queued entry SHALL be created against it, the message MUST NOT be re-homed, and an `agent_action_rejected` event SHALL be recorded. The MCP send tool SHALL carry the same three parts.

WhyAn agent whose send is refused should not have to reconstruct a message from context it has already moved past.

FR-16# Requirement 9 of 9

SHALL A trigger naming a conversation that cannot be continued SHALL be refused with 409 and a sentence distinguishing the cause: unknown in this project, belonging to another agent (naming it), or archived (advising to unarchive it), each also saying that omitting `conversation_id` starts a new conversation.

WhyOne generic sentence for a typo, another agent's thread and an archived one named no repair.

Design

D1. Truncation is the floor and is applied when the first message is queued (`name_conversation`, a no-op once titled); pre-existing untitled rows are named lazily on listing rather than by a migration.

D2. Generation is a best-effort upgrade that records an event, not a run: a run row under the agent's name would gate the scheduler and stall the agent's queue.

D3. Operator-set titles are a flag (`title_set_by_operator`), checked before and after generation so a rename made while the model runs wins.

D4. Archive refuses instead of resolving: it never stops a run or drops a queued entry. Unarchive is never refused.

D5. Origin is a required argument of `new_conversation`, not a default.

  • hub/tests/test_conversation_archive.py, test_conversation_archive_refusal.py (round trip, listings, counts, refusals)

Limits

  • The `spec` origin is declared in `CONVERSATION_ORIGINS` (hub/hub/db/models.py:403) but no code creates a conversation with it; the spec workspace reuses the agent's conversation.
