Requirements

FR-1# Requirement 1 of 9

SHALL The Hub SHALL create a `spec_documents` row for a specification file that exists in the project's `spec/` tree and has no row, deriving the document's identity from the file's own content. The adopted document MUST thereafter be indistinguishable from one created through the Hub, so that phase transitions, requirement indexing, coverage, evidence and task materialisation all operate on it.

FR-2# Requirement 2 of 9

SHALL Adoption SHALL be read-only on disk. No adoption path may write, truncate, rename or delete a specification file, whatever the outcome of the adoption.

FR-3# Requirement 3 of 9

SHALL Adoption SHALL take the adopted document's phase from the `aw-spec-status` metadata the renderer writes into the file, because the payload block does not carry it. Where that metadata is absent, names no known phase, or names a phase the document's kind may not be in, adoption SHALL fall back to the phase a newly created document of that kind would receive: `current` for a `capability` document, `exploring` otherwise.

FR-4# Requirement 4 of 9

SHALL Adoption SHALL refuse a file from which no payload block can be read, rather than inventing an identity for it. The Hub has no title or kind for such a file, and a guessed name would enter a file that outlives the machine that guessed it.

FR-5# Requirement 5 of 9

MUST Where the adopted path already has a `spec_documents` row, adoption SHALL refuse and SHALL report every field on which the file and the row disagree. Adoption MUST NOT update an existing row from a file: resolving that disagreement is a separate operator decision.

FR-6# Requirement 6 of 9

SHALL The Hub SHALL offer adoption of every adoptable document in the project's `spec/` tree in a single operation, so that a project whose database was created after its files — a clone, a migration, or a restored machine — is recoverable without one call per document.

FR-7# Requirement 7 of 9

SHALL Adoption SHALL resolve every path through the project workspace and SHALL refuse any path that escapes the project's `spec/` tree, on the same terms as existing document discovery.

FR-8# Requirement 8 of 9

SHALL Once adopted, a document SHALL be filed into `spec/index.json` by the existing reindex operation, carrying its real title and kind rather than being reported as unindexable.

FR-9# Requirement 9 of 9

SHALL Where the operator's document browser lists a specification file that exists on disk and has no document record, the interface SHALL offer to adopt it there, and SHALL offer to adopt every such file at once.

WhyA clone, a restore or a second machine produces a corpus whose files predate its records. The browser already shows those files; showing them without a way to track them leaves the operator looking at documents the Hub cannot move through any phase. Each refusal or skip SHALL be shown with the reason the Hub gave, including the disagreements an already-tracked path reports, and a corpus-wide adoption SHALL show its discovery diagnostics, so a truncated sweep is not presented as a complete one.
