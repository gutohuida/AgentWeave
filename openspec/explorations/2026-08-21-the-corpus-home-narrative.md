# Exploration — The corpus home's narrative is still thin (2026-08-21)

**Status:** OPEN. Carries `corpus-aware-documents` **6.6**, waived when that change was archived.

## What was done, and what was not

`corpus-aware-documents` gave the home a **generated map** — six areas, each with its capabilities
nested underneath, rebuilt on reindex. That shipped and was verified: the map renders, navigates,
resolves offline, and a reader does not mistake it for prose (8.1–8.6).

The map is not the narrative. Task 6.6 says so explicitly: *"the 'thin' complaint is about this
prose, and the generated map does not replace it."* The authored summary above the map is still
what it was.

## The separate content backlog (6.7)

Eight documents have no usable summary and render as gaps in the map. That task carries its own
instruction — *"Do not treat writing them as part of this change's completion"* — and it was waived
on those terms. It is content work, not a defect, and it is tracked here so it is not lost.

## Open questions

1. What is the home's narrative actually *for*, given the map now answers "what is in here"?
   Plausibly: the invariants that hold across the product and that no single capability owns —
   which is what the current summary already gestures at.
2. Who writes it? This is prose about the whole system, so it is the one document an agent is
   least well placed to draft alone.
3. Do the eight summary-less documents get written, or retired? A capability document nobody can
   summarise may be describing something that no longer exists.
