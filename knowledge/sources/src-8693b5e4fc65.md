# Check and the agent's save both say "ready", but approval then finds gaps neither showed

Recorded 2026-10-29 by Architect at the operator's request, to fix later. Not yet in
scripts/drive/FINDINGS.md.

## What happened

Change document `spec/changes/a-checks-send-back-returns-the-task-s-accepted-evidence`
(spdoc-ed413679f369). Every agent save answered `blocking: []` and `ready_to_approve: true`, and
the operator's **Check** button agreed. The operator then went to approve and was shown gaps
neither had reported:

- `criterion_without_check` ×12: no criterion had `how_to_check` or `checked_by`.
- `steps_skipped`: the document was still at step `intake` of the small journey
  (requirements-and-acceptance, tasks, build).

The agent fixed both in two calls once it could see them. The cost was a round trip through the
operator, who had been told twice that the document was ready.

## Why (master)

- `spec_service.check` (hub/hub/spec_service.py, ~1494) returns "exactly as the agent's last save
  of the stored payload reported it": `_completeness_report` over the payload. The Check route
  (`POST /documents/check`, hub/hub/api/v1/spec.py ~3056) calls it.
- `spec_service.phase_findings` (~1459) runs only for `to_phase == approved`. It adds what "only
  proposal and approval report":
  - `spec_completeness.approval_gaps`: `criterion_without_check`, and `no_acceptance_drive`
    for change-specs;
  - `overlap_warnings` (F694) and `undeclared_files_warnings` (F1014);
  - `_steps_skipped`;
  - `_amendments_unreviewed`;
  - `_vault_warnings`.
- `approval_gaps`' docstring says they are kept out of `check` on purpose ("because the save
  result is unchanged by this change; the acceptance step's own `spec_journey.missing` already
  asks the author for the check fields"). In practice an author who fills the document in one
  submission without walking the steps never sees `spec_journey.missing`. The `ready_to_approve`
  field then says true when approval will not be clean.

## Expected (direction, not decided)

- Check shows everything approval would show. At minimum it shows the approval-only gaps, as
  gaps the operator can approve over, beside the blocking list.
- The agent's save result does not say `ready_to_approve: true` while approval would report gaps.
  It either includes them, or names them as "approval will also ask about …".
- A test: a payload with a criterion lacking how_to_check, at step intake. Save and Check each
  report `criterion_without_check` and `steps_skipped` (or say approval will), and agree with
  what approval reports.

  only when that field is true". Here the field itself was wrong.
